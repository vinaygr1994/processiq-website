What We Build

A Salesforce org that fights your team is worse than no CRM at all. We design architectures that match how your salespeople actually work — not generic templates.

  • Custom object & field architecture aligned to your sales process
  • Page layout design for maximum rep efficiency
  • Record types for multi-product or multi-segment sales motions
  • Permission sets & profiles — right data, right people
  • Validation rules that enforce data quality without friction
  • Opportunity stage mapping with probability weighting
  • Lead & contact object design with source attribution
  • Account hierarchy setup for enterprise sales

Why Architecture Matters

Most Salesforce orgs are built reactively — a field added here, a layout changed there — until the org becomes a patchwork that no one trusts.

We start from your revenue motion and design backwards. Every object, field, and relationship has a purpose: to give your team a clear picture of every deal and your leadership a reliable forecast.

Result: 98% CRM data accuracy and a system your reps actually use.
