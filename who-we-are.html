We're a specialist B2B revenue architecture firm — bridging the gap between high-end consulting and cutting-edge technical engineering with 5+ years of enterprise experience.

Our Story

Process IQ Automation was founded on a simple observation: most B2B companies invest heavily in CRM, paid acquisition, and automation tools — but keep them in separate silos. The result is fragmented data, poor attribution, and a revenue team that can't answer the most basic question: where is our pipeline coming from?

We built Process IQ to solve that. Our team combines deep Salesforce engineering expertise, paid media architecture, and RPA automation into a unified practice — so your entire revenue stack works as one engine, not three disconnected tools.

Based in Toronto, Ontario, we work with B2B companies across North America — from Series A startups to 500-person enterprises — who are serious about building revenue infrastructure that scales.

Our Values

  • Transparency first — We share what we find in discovery, even when it's not what you want to hear. Honest audits lead to better architectures.
  • No vendor lock-in — Everything we build is yours. Fully documented, fully transferable, no proprietary platforms.
